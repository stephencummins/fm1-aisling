The controls
White keysPlay the Lead (the flute), in the piece's scale (the key it sounds is lit)
Black keysEleven effects, each while held (lit; dark while held): Ceo, Macalla, Fras, Repeat, Shimmer, Freeze, Octave, Return, then Rise, Close, Transpose (in PERFORM: the solos)
PLAY · REC · EDITPlay / rest · a tap: hold (the echo repeats what it holds), held: the effects grid (16 effects on the white keys) · a tap: the Chords page, held: the next chord
SELPERFORM: the knobs Filter, Wet, Energy, Sweep; white 1–5 mute the Drone, Sequence, Lead, Sequence 2, Atmosphere; black 1–5 solo them; white 6 held: the levels; white 7–11 held: Sweep, Open, Shut, Drop (both sequences out), Echo; the top key (G): the Lead forgets its ideas and its theme. Held: Lock
HOMEThe Aisling page (Filter, Evolve, Motion, Drift); again, the Key page (Tempo, Root, Mode, Transpose)
ENVThe Drone (Pedal, Reso, Phaser, Attack); again, its sound (Colour, Release, Reverb, Echo); again, its Move page (Width, Time, Rate, Intro)
ARPThe Sequence (Density, Octave, Length, Grid); again, its sound (Cutoff, Decay, Reverb, Echo); again, its Repeats (Repeat A, A time, Repeat B, B time); again, its Move page
LFOThe Lead (Phrases, Octave, Glide, Vibrato); again, its sound (Tone, Attack, Reverb, Echo); again, its Move page
SEQSequence 2 (Chance, Octave, Length, Level); again, its sound; again, the Pattern page (write the steps on the keys); again, its Move page
SAVEThe Atmosphere (Density, Width, Drift, Air); again, its sound (Colour, Ring, Reverb, Echo); again, its Move page
FXSpace (Echo, Echo time, Feedback, Reverb); again, the Echo page (Blur, Tone, Width, Size)
GLOSetup (Tune, MIDI out, Lights, Save); again, the Arc (Lead in, Bridge, Arc, Memory). Held: save; held on: the piece back to its recipe
ALGORITHMOn a layer's page, its voice (the Atmosphere: its character)
PRESETSThe piece
SELECTOn a layer's pages, its level; on the Aisling page, Energy (elsewhere nothing)
OCT− / OCT+On the Sequence's, the Lead's and Sequence 2's pages, its octave (lit while it's above or below where the piece was loaded)
