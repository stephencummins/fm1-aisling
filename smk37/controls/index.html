The pads and the faders

  • The 16 pads: PERFORM's effects, on any page, each on while its pad is down. Top row: Stutter, Half time, Gate, Roll, Repeat, Macalla, Tape stop, Reverse. Bottom row: Reverb, Shimmer, Freeze, Wash, Flange, Phaser, Drive, Crush.
  • The pads' lights: each glows in its group's colour: orange for the rhythm effects, sea green for the echoes, heather for the space, yellow for the colour. Bright while held, beating while latched, dark asleep.
  • > held, a pad hit: that effect stays on; the same again lets it go. > is lit while any is latched.
  • < tapped: every pad and latch let go.
  • A pad down, K5 to K8 turned: a throw of Filter, Wet, Energy or Duck, which go back when the last pad is up.
  • The 4 faders: the mix, left to right: the Drone, the Sequence, Sequence 2 and the Lead. With FADER BANK lit: Filter, Wet, Energy and Duck. A fader does nothing until it is moved. The mix is saved with the piece; Filter, Wet, Energy and Duck are not.

Not used yet: the pitch and mod wheels, the pedal, KNOB BANK, the outer keys and Bluetooth.

What the Elite doesn't have

  • Lit keys. The FM-1 lights the key the Lead sounds, and the black keys (dark while held); the Elite's keys have no lights. Its buttons do, and AISLING uses them as on the FM-1.
  • MIDI in on the TRS jack. On the Elite, MIDI comes in over USB only.
