controls · AISLING on the M-VAVE FM-1

cheat sheet

Your FM-1's colours:

The drawing alone, at full size.

In short
White keysPlay the Lead (the piece's flute, fiddle or voice), in the piece's scale (the key it sounds is lit)
Black keysEleven effects, each while held (lit; dark while held): Ceo, Macalla, Fras, Repeat, Shimmer, Freeze, Octave, Return, then Rise, Close, Transpose (in PERFORM: the solos)
PLAY · REC · EDITPlay / rest · a tap: hold (the echo repeats what it holds), held: the effects grid (16 effects on the white keys) · a tap: the Chords page, held: the next chord
SELPERFORM (again: back to the page you came from): the knobs Filter, Wet, Energy, Chorus; every one of the 27 keys an effect on the whole music while held (the white keys in fours: Rhythm, Echo, Space, Colour); SAVE tapped there: the Lead forgets its ideas and its theme. Held: Lock
HOMEThe Aisling page (Filter, Evolve, Motion, Drift; ALGORITHM, Evolve's timing); again, the Key page (Tempo, Root, Mode, Transpose). Held, with OCT− or OCT+: undo or redo
ENVThe Drone (Pedal, Reso, Phaser, Attack); again, its sound (Colour, Release, Reverb, Echo); again, its Move page (Width, Time, Rate, Intro)
ARPThe Sequence (Density, Octave, Length, Grid); again, its sound (Cutoff, Decay, Reverb, Echo); again, its Repeats (Repeat A, A time, Repeat B, B time); again, its Move page
LFOThe Lead (Phrases, Octave, Ornament, Vibrato); again, its sound (Tone, Attack, Reverb, Echo); again, its style (Ornament, Glide, Vibrato, Memory); again, its Move page
SEQSequence 2 (Chance, Octave, Length, Level); again, its sound; again, the Pattern page (write the steps on the keys); again, its Move page
SAVEThe Atmosphere (Density, Width, Drift, Air); again, its sound (Colour, Ring, Reverb, Echo); again, its Move page. Held: save
FXSpace (Echo, Echo time, Feedback, Reverb); again, the Echo page (Blur, Tone, Width, Size)
GLOSetup (Tune, MIDI out, Lights, Save; ALGORITHM, the sleep timer); again, the Arc (Lead in, Bridge, Arc, Memory); again, the Journey (Piece, In set, Day; SELECT, the stay on each piece). Held: save; held on: the piece back to its recipe
ALGORITHMOn a layer's page, its voice (the Atmosphere: its character); on the Aisling page, Evolve's timing; on the Setup page, the sleep timer
PRESETSThe piece
SELECTOn a layer's pages, its level; on the Aisling page, Energy (elsewhere nothing)
OCT− / OCT+On the Sequence's, the Lead's and Sequence 2's pages, its octave (lit while it's above or below where the piece was loaded)

The manual has the rest; the cheat sheet draws the steps as flows, then this table with each control as it looks, to print on one sheet.
