BLACK KEYS: ELEVEN MORE EFFECTS
SAVE, TAPPED: FORGET. THE LEAD THINKS OF A NEW THEME
WHITE 1-4: RHYTHM
Stutter: an eighth caught, round and round. Half time: the sequences at half speed. Gate: chopped on the sixteenths. Roll: the notes doubled.
WHITE 5-8: ECHO
Repeat: the Sequence thrown into the echo. Macalla: the echo rings on and builds. Tape stop: the tape slowing. Reverse: each eighth backwards.
WHITE 9-12: SPACE
Reverb: the dry music into the plate. Shimmer: the plate climbing in octaves. Freeze: the cloud holds. Wash: all into the echo and the plate.
WHITE 13-16: COLOUR
Flange: a jet passing. Phaser: deep, on all the music. Drive: warm, growing as you hold. Crush: gritty and bright.

The black keys
Ceo: fog. Fras: a burst of rain. Sweep: the Sequence's filter swept. Rise, Close: every filter opened, closed. Octave: the Sequence up. Dive: the pitch falls. Ring: bells. Vinyl: an old record. Comb: a ringing pipe. Drop: both sequences out.
