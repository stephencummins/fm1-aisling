aisling · generative Irish ambient for M-VAVE's FM-1 and SMK-37 Elite

by CU_STE · www.stephen8n.com

AISLING (said ASH-ling: Irish for a dream or a vision) is a generative instrument after the harpers and the west of Ireland: a harp's ostinato over a held drone, a second harp drifting against it, a flute far back in the reverb, with surf, wind or rain behind. It was written for the M-VAVE FM-1, and runs on M-VAVE's SMK-37 Elite too; AISLING FX-1 turns an FM-1 into an effects box for a computer's sound. Which have you got?

FM-1

v1.4 · released

The instrument: eight pieces that play themselves for hours, the flute on the white keys, the effects on the black ones. Install it from Chrome or Edge, or try it here without an FM-1.
